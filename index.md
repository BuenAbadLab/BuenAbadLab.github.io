The BuenAbad Lab develops computational and statistical methods to understand how RNA processing — especially alternative splicing — shapes gene expression, genome function, and human disease. We work across scales, from single cells to population-scale genomics, integrating transcriptomic, genomic, and multi-omic data to uncover the regulatory logic of the transcriptome and its role in health and disease.

We are part of the [Clemson University Institute for Human Genetics](https://www.clemson.edu/science/departments/genetics-biochemistry/institute-human-genetics/) in Greenwood, South Carolina, and are affiliated with the Department of Genetics and Biochemistry.
